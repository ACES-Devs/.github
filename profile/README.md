# ACES DEVELOPER COMMITTEE

> Engineering digital solutions, empowering innovators, and driving technology across the department.

The **ACES Developer Committee** is the official technical and development arm of the **Association of Computer E-Students (ACES)**—the head student council of the Computer Studies Department at the **University of Caloocan City – Congressional Campus**.

We are dedicated to developing, and deploying internal digital infrastructure, student portals, and automated systems that support council operations and serve the entire student body.

<div style="height: 1px; width: 100%; background-color: #30363d; margin: 16px 0;"></div>

## About the Committee

The **ACES Developer Committee** serves as the technical engine of the Association of Computer E-Students, dedicated to advancing digital innovation across the Computer Studies Department at the University of Caloocan City – Congressional Campus.

Our mission is to engineer purposeful, student-first technology solutions that modernize council operations, streamline campus events, and provide accessible digital platforms for the student community. Beyond building software, the committee acts as an incubator for aspiring software developers — fostering technical mastery, collaborative problem-solving, and adherence to industry-standard software development lifecycles (SDLC) from ideation to deployment.
